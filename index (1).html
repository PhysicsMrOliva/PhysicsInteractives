Physics Interactives

Simulations and animations for exploring motion. Change the starting conditions, press Play, and watch the position, velocity and acceleration graphs build in step with the motion.

Interactive labs

Open in any browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

Constant-acceleration lab

Motion in one dimension with a cart on a ramp, a fan cart on a level track, or an object in free fall. Set the initial position, velocity and acceleration, then compare the x-t, v-t and a-t graphs.

Open the lab

Projectile motion lab

Launch a ball at any speed, angle and height. The horizontal and vertical motions are graphed side by side, showing constant vₓ and steadily changing vᵧ.

Open the lab

Velocity vs. displacement lab

A fan cart speeds up along a level track. Graph velocity against displacement, see vf = √(2aΔx) worked out live, and switch to v² to turn the curve into a straight line with slope 2a.

Open the lab

Two-part motion lab

Sprinters, cars, bicycles, rockets, dropped pumpkins and cannonballs with two sections of constant acceleration. Set each part, then solve them one at a time: Part 2 starts with Part 1’s final position and velocity.

Open the lab

Broom ball force lab

Push a bowling ball with a broom: start it from rest, let it coast, then slow it and reverse it. Connect the force direction to the acceleration and compare with the lab video’s data.

Open the lab

Newton’s Law Blocks: explorer

Follow-up to the Newton’s Law Blocks lab. Every rubber band pulls with the same force and every block has the same mass, so you can reason with ratios: a = F / m. Two bands → 2× the force. Two blocks → 2× the mass.
